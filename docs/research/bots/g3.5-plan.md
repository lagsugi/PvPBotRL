# G3.5 fine-tuning plan

`g3.5` is the candidate name for a continuation of `g3-prototype` on the corrected Paper Citizens simulator. The goal is to keep the Prototype's ability to open and extend combos while learning to recover when opponents deny its first hit, interrupt its follow-up, or change their spacing and attack timing. Bot vs Bot is the primary acceptance test. Player vs Bot provides supplementary observations. The campaign ends with a candidate and an acceptance report. Bundling and Paper registration are separate decisions.

In the November 27 fixed-equipment Citizens round robin, the Prototype won 81.55% against Pressure and 85.71% against Orbit, compared with 30.95% against G2 and 36.90% against G2+. Each matchup used 168 games on a fixed scenario grid. These results motivate giving strong recurring opponents and opponents that change tactics a substantial share of training. The round robin report is stored locally at `build/g3/paper-league-20261127/fixed-only/report.md`. [Previous G3 campaign and Paper findings](g3.md)

## Training implementation

- Initialize from `build/g3/full-780m/training/candidate-0018983.pt`. Verify its SHA-256 and the bundled Prototype JSON against the committed provenance before starting. Transfer the `.pt` to that path on the selected host. Load the `g3` actor, normalization, GRU, critic, and forecast head with a fresh Adam optimizer, random streams, and league. Compare the initial export with the bundled Prototype on recurrent logits, memory, and selected actions. Keep the 48-feature schema, its masks and action order, and the existing deployment JSON format. [Prototype provenance](../../../models/bundled/g3_prototype.source.json)
- Add an explicit `paper_citizens` mode to Rust rollouts and paired evaluation, using Minecraft 26.1.2 and `sword_pvp_fixed_equipment_v1`. Share the existing Citizens tick progression between the normal policy callback and the learning batch. Run the opponent's decision at its actual position in the tick, and pause for Python only when the learner is due. Return the learner's observation, action mask, transition reward, and terminal outcome through the batch interface. Preserve same-tick damage and knockback for the second decision, delayed opponent information, alternating lead order, and the actual previous submitted action. Collect one PPO sample per submitted learner decision. Attribute the interval from that decision to the next decision or terminal state to the submitted action. Reset learner and opponent memory and previous action at episode start. Preserve the legacy driver defaults and replay compatibility. [Citizens driver and parity fixture](../../simulator/README.md#paper-citizens-bot-vs-bot)
- Keep G3's terminal win and HP reward, HP and approach potentials, exchange-overlap cost, time and timeout costs, and delivered-HP forecast loss. Disable the cold-start imitation guide. Optimize wins and favorable exchanges with the existing objective. Measure movement variety through evaluation of profitable exchanges. Use 384 lanes, 128 decisions per lane per rollout, sequence length 32, burn-in up to 8, 4,096 minibatch tokens, learning rate `1e-4`, up to three PPO epochs, and a KL target of 0.018. Use the existing seeded spawn and seat variation and a 6,000-tick cap. Sample Mixed RTT for 40% of training matches and draw uniformly among the other seven final network conditions for the remainder. [G3 objective and architecture](g3.md#design)

### Opponent mix

| Training opponent pool | Target share |
| --- | ---: |
| Frozen, bundled `g3-prototype` | 20% |
| Frozen G2 and G2+ | 25% |
| Earlier `g3.5` snapshots | 25% |
| Opponents that change tactics during the duel | 20% |
| Other fixed opponents | 10% |

Sample G2 and G2+ equally and use Recurrent PPO, Critical+, Pressure, and Counter for the other fixed pool. Until a learner snapshot exists, assign its share equally to G2 and G2+. Once snapshots are available, divide self-play equally between the latest four and the older collection, with uniform sampling within each group. Use all available snapshots when there is no older group. Each ongoing episode retains its original opponent weights and controller state.

The switching pool draws equally from Pressure↔Counter and Orbit↔Critical+, starts in either style with equal probability, and switches at seeded intervals of 48–96 server ticks. Its controller receives ordinary opponent observations and submits ordinary actions. Review re-entry after a denied opening, escape and counterattack after consecutive incoming hits, and recovery when a retreating opponent punishes the chase in development replays.

Run both pilots from the same parent and training seed. The control moves the switching pool's 20% into the other fixed pool. Give both pilots the same update cap, sized from measured throughput to fit 35 minutes of training each, and reserve the remaining ten minutes per pilot for development evaluation. Save a self-play snapshot every 15 minutes during the pilots. A pilot qualifies for selection if it meets the common-opponent win, HP, clean-exchange, and timeout margins below using point estimates on the same development seeds. Rank qualifying pilots by the development score below. Continue the fixed-opponent control on a tie or when neither qualifies, and record the reason. Continue the selected pilot from its own checkpoint and save atomic resume checkpoints every five minutes and candidate exports plus matching checkpoints every 30 minutes. Resume restores the optimizer, RNG, and league and starts fresh episodes.

## Evaluation and acceptance

Use separate seed families for hardware measurement, training, development, and final evaluation. For development, use 16 seat pairs per cell on Mixed, Both20, and Asymmetric against the Prototype and G2+ directly and against Recurrent PPO, G2, and Critical+ as common opponents. Evaluate the Prototype as a common-opponent control. Use Pressure↔Counter with a continued-style control as a development probe for tactical response. Score the regular cells by the equal-cell candidate mean of win rate + 0.35 × win-weighted HP fraction + 0.15 × profitable opening diversity − 0.12 × even-exchange fraction − timeout rate. Use the same point-estimate margins as the pilots and rank up to eight evenly spaced exports and the last verified export. If no export meets the development margins, evaluate the highest-scoring export and record that choice. Freeze one policy hash and its matching checkpoint before opening final results.

For the final matrix, use 128 independent seat pairs per cell. Swap policies while keeping physical placements, lead order, RTT assignments, and seed fixed. The eight network conditions are Mixed, symmetric 0, 2, 20, 60, 120, and 150 ms, and asymmetric 0/150 ms. Use deterministic evaluation actions. Compute equal-cell means and marginal 95% intervals with 2,000 bootstrap resamples stratified by cell. Keep both seats and all policies and branches within a seed pair together in the resample.

- **Direct strength.** Require a win-rate interval lower bound above 50% against each of `g3-prototype` and G2+. Report direct results against G2 and Recurrent PPO as additional controls. Include timeouts in the win-rate denominator.
- **Generalization.** The six common opponents for the candidate and Prototype are Recurrent PPO, G2, G2+, Critical+, held-out Reach, and the held-out G4 challenger. Require a lower bound of at least −2 percentage points for common-opponent win gain and at least −0.5 HP for win-weighted remaining HP gain. Count losses and timeouts as zero HP for this metric. Require the win-rate difference lower bound to be at least −5 percentage points separately against Reach and the G4 challenger, and the overall candidate timeout rate to stay below 1%.
- **Profitable movement variety.** Use the G3 eight-decision exchange window and its six opening movement classes. For each match, measure the probability that two distinct profitable windows start in different classes, with zero for matches with fewer than two profitable windows. Require the lower bound on diversity gain to exceed zero and the lower bound on clean-exchange gain to be at least −2 percentage points on the common-opponent matrix. Report repeated even exchanges alongside these results. [G3 exchange and diversity definitions](g3.md#evaluation)
- **Tactical response.** Reserve Orbit↔Pressure for the final test. For each direction and seed, pick a switch tick uniformly from 40–79. Reproduce the same pre-switch trajectory for each tested policy, then compare switching that tick with continuing the original style. Score dealt damage minus damage received over the following 24 server ticks. HP stays at its terminal value if a branch ends early. Use seeds where both policies and both seat assignments reach the switch. Report eligible pairs, post-switch movement and swing choices, and results by direction. Require at least 64 eligible seed pairs per direction in aggregate. Require a 95% lower bound above zero both for the candidate's switched-branch net HP gain over the Prototype and for the difference in each policy's switched-minus-continued net HP, pooling directions equally.
