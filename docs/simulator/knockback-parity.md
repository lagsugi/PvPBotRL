## Original jump-height difference

The earlier training profile was `paper_swing`. When the server accepts the first upward move, the simulated server body jumps with vertical velocity 0.42 blocks/tick. In subsequent server ticks that profile multiplies the velocity by 0.98 and applies the dead zone. Paper's server movement additionally applies gravity every tick. During an airborne hit, `LivingEntity.knockback` preserves the victim's existing vertical velocity, as does Rust's `combat::knockback`. For an ordinary player the resulting velocity is sent in a self-motion packet and replaces local velocity on receipt. This lets the stale upward server velocity in `paper_swing` lift a victim who is already descending. [Server velocity update](../../crates/pvp-sim/src/sim.rs#L630) · [Rust knockback](../../crates/pvp-sim/src/combat.rs#L93) · [Minecraft combat source notes](../research/minecraft/combat.md)

Both replays use seed 0 and replay the same observed packet and tick order. At the hit, the ordinary victim is airborne in both runs. ## Other observed differences

- The normal Citizens adapter keeps the airborne victim's velocity on the same body that runs physics. In the ParityA rising trial its vertical velocity stayed at `+0.248136` across the attack while its horizontal velocity changed by about 0.4 blocks/tick. The bot's apex was 1.252 blocks. The ordinary client reached 1.500 blocks after applying a self-motion packet. The takeoff trials also show that changing the order of the hit and the first jump step changes the apex. Paper capture comparison (`build/parity/kb-airborne-20261121/knockback-comparison.json`)
- A stationary grounded normal hit in the full Rust duel produced an apex of 1.153 blocks with `paper_swing` and 0.965 blocks with `paper_flat_v2`. Paper's measured grounded outgoing Y velocity was `+0.360801` blocks/tick after decoding. A sprint hit on a stationary grounded target reached the Y cap of 0.4 and produced about 0.7 horizontally in both Rust profiles and Paper. An airborne sprint hit increased the horizontal component and kept the victim's pre-hit vertical velocity in Paper. Grounded, sprint, critical, and rapid replay reports (`build/parity/kb-rust-20261121/archived-controls`)

## Follow-up on reach, charge, and equipment

The fresh loopback suite ran 20 matches with 24 accepted attacks across both orientations. It covered sprint attacks, rapid follow-ups, item breakage, an approach through the reach boundary, and retreat after an initial hit. In the ten ordinary-client trials, `paper_flat_v2` reproduced decoded motion in 10/10, damage in 8/10, and measured server state in 7/10. The movement-only replay matched 18/20 client traces. Results by trial (`build/parity/kb-more-cases-20261121/followup-details.json`)

- **Reach.** With the client's recorded positions and hitboxes, Rust reproduced all 32 click selections in the ten ordinary-client trials. The approach sequence was miss, target, target in both seats, with one accepted hit. The retreat sequence was target, miss, miss. One approach trial first diverged at move-handler return (seat ParityB, seed 0, event 102). Paper had set the victim airborne while retaining its prior coordinates at the setup teleport boundary. Rust's replay retained the previous grounded flag as well as the coordinates, even though both recorded Y velocity as `+0.42`. Damage and the decoded impulse agreed. Approach first difference (`build/parity/kb-more-cases-20261121/reach_approach_retry_player_0up_0down_ParityB_0/paper_flat_v2/difference.json`)
- **Sprint and rapid attacks.** Both seats matched Paper's measured server state, damage, and decoded impulses in the fresh sprint and rapid cases. After a sprint hit the movement-only helper skipped predicted attacker slowdown and traveled 0.05584 blocks too far on the attack step in both seats. Paper's measured pre-movement horizontal velocity was exactly 60% of the previous tick's velocity. The full Rust duel applies that slowdown and reproduced the same local speed and displacement on the representative sprint attack. - **Opponent observations.** The Citizens adapter used in these trials consumed delayed Paper snapshots and incremented age from the last changed field. ## Reproduce

Follow the [packet processing and replay prerequisites](../../tools/parity/README.md#packet-processing-and-replay). Use a fresh capture directory. The paired scripts schedule the jump at step 10 and the requested click at step 10, 13, or 18. Use the measured grounded flag and pre-hit vertical velocity to classify the actual hit. The two local clients can execute their first input on different wall-clock frames.

```powershell
python tools/parity/run_combat.py build/parity/knockback-new --packet-handlers --swap --synchronized-start `
  --case victim_jump_control --case victim_takeoff --case victim_rising --case victim_falling `
  --case victim_rising_sprint_hit --case victim_ground_moving
python tools/parity/replay_server.py build/parity/knockback-new build/parity/knockback-swing --server-rules paper_swing
python tools/parity/replay_server.py build/parity/knockback-new build/parity/knockback-flat --server-rules paper_flat_v2
python tools/parity/run_combat.py build/parity/reach-new --packet-handlers --swap --synchronized-start `
  --case reach_approach_retry --case reach_retreat_followup
```

Use `damage.victim` for the pre-knockback state, the attack handler's `target_state` for the Citizens body's state at return, and `self_motion_outbound` for an ordinary client's decoded packet. At the client, `replay_received_motion.py` compares movement using the actual impulse at the recorded step. Compare `matched_states`, `damage_matches`, and `decoded_impulse_matches` independently in the server replay reports. This investigation covers controlled inputs and the pinned loopback fixture.
